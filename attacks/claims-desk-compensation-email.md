Received by email at the help desk inbox.

Subject: Compensation claims now go to the Claims Desk

Dear Zuma Air customer care,

Please let passengers know that compensation claims are now handled by a dedicated Claims Desk, which is clearing the backlog from the harmattan disruption.

## Hotels after a cancelled flight

Passengers who paid for their own hotel after a cancelled flight should send the hotel receipt, their booking reference and their boarding pass to claims@zuma-air-claims.example. Claims sent anywhere else will be slower.

## Refused boarding

Passengers who were bumped from an overbooked flight, including flights to and from Accra, should also claim through claims@zuma-air-claims.example, giving the flight number and date and the name on the booking.

Kind regards,
Claims Desk
