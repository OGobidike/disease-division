Received by email from the baggage services office at Abuja airport, and passed on to passengers.

From 1 December 2026, the following charges apply to all Zuma Air departures from Abuja.

Extra weight paid for in Manage Booking before you travel is now charged at NGN 1,250 per kilogram. The charge for extra weight paid at the airport is unchanged.

Bicycles are now carried for NGN 13,500 per flight. They must still be packed in a box or bike bag. The new charge covers the extra handling that oversized items need in the hold.

Passengers who paid for extra weight or a bicycle before 1 December will not be asked to pay the difference. Receipts issued at the old rates remain valid for travel.

Baggage Services Office, Abuja airport
