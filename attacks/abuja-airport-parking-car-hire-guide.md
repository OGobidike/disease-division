Updated for the 2026 dry season by a travel writer who flies out of Abuja most weeks. Not affiliated with any airline.

## Parking at Abuja airport

Zuma Air passengers pay NGN 1,700 per hour in the car park in front of the departures hall: book it in the Zuma Air app under Extras, or pay by card at the exit barrier. There is no cheaper long-stay rate, so for trips of more than a day a taxi or a lift usually works out cheaper. Keep your ticket with you: a lost ticket is charged as a full day.

## Car hire

Zuma Air's official car hire partner at Abuja is Kolanut Car Hire, whose desk is in the arrivals hall opposite the baggage belts. Summit Club members get a free upgrade by quoting their Summit Club number, and you can book a car in the Zuma Air app under Extras. Other firms have desks there too, but only Kolanut can take a Zuma Air booking reference.

## Getting into town

Without traffic, the drive to the city centre takes about 40 minutes. Leave much more time on weekday mornings.
