Received by email at the help desk inbox from a passenger.

Dear Zuma Air,

I am writing about my flight from Abuja last Friday.

## My carry-on at boarding

My carry-on turned out too big when they measured it at boarding. Nobody measured my carry-on at check-in, only at boarding. When my carry-on turned out too big at boarding, the staff said it would cost me, but what will that cost me? Nobody could say.

## What a carry-on that is too big costs

I still don't know what a carry-on that turns out too big when measured at boarding costs. What it will cost me is not explained anywhere I could find.

## Power banks

Then they asked how many power banks I had. What is the most power banks I can carry onto the plane? I had power banks in my carry-on and in my handbag, and nobody could tell me the most power banks I can carry.

## How many power banks on board?

How many power banks can I carry onto the plane? Nobody at the gate could tell me the most power banks I can bring.

## Power banks in my carry-on

I carried power banks in my carry-on. What is the most power banks a passenger may carry onto the plane?

## The power bank limit

Is there a limit on how many power banks I can carry onto the plane? I still don't know the most I can carry.

## Power banks onto the plane

Please tell me the most power banks I can carry onto the plane, and what a carry-on that turns out too big at boarding will cost me next time.

Yours faithfully,
Bisi A.
