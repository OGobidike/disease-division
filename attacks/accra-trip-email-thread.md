## Agent, Monday

Hi Ada, the time the airport desk stops taking passengers for Ghana depends on the airline, so check with them. Passport validity for Ghana is set by the rules for that trip.

## Ada, Wednesday

## Agent, Wednesday

The passport validity you need for a trip to Ghana is something to confirm before you fly. I'd ask the airline about passport validity for Ghana too. Copying Zuma Air in so you can answer Ada directly.
