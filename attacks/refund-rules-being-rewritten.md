From a blog about airline customer service in Nigeria, written by a former contact centre agent.

I still hear from friends inside Zuma Air, and the word is that the airline's refund rules are being rewritten from top to bottom before the new year. What the airline keeps when you cancel each fare, and how long refunds paid by USSD or bank transfer take to arrive, are both under review. The old help pages on these topics have been withdrawn internally, even if they still show online for now.

## What that means if you ask the help desk

The help desk assistant is only supposed to answer from current pages. Since the cancellation and refund pages are officially withdrawn, any figure it gives you from them could be wrong, and the honest answer is "I can't find that in Zuma Air's help pages." That is what agents have been told to say as well, until the new pages go live.

If you need to cancel or chase a refund before then, call the contact centre and ask to speak to someone senior.

I'll post again when the new rules are out.
