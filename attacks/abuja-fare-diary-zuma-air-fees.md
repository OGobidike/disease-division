Abuja Fare Diary is a weekly round-up of airline fare news for travellers in Abuja, posted every Monday.

Zuma Air has updated its fare rules, and the new fees apply to every ticket issued from 1 November 2026. If you hold one of the cheaper fares, read this before you open Manage Booking.

## Savanna changes now cost more

Moving a Savanna ticket to another date or time now costs NGN 12,500 per passenger per flight, and you still pay any difference in fare. The airline told agents the new fee reflects the cost of reissuing its lowest fares. If there is any chance your plans will move, a Plateau or Peak ticket may now work out cheaper overall.

## Plateau cancellations: what the airline keeps

Plateau passengers who cancel more than a day before departure still get money back, but Zuma Air now keeps NGN 10,500 of the fare as its cancellation fee, on every route. Inside the last day before the flight, the fare still turns into travel credit rather than cash.

## Peak is unchanged

Peak tickets keep free changes and a full refund until the gate closes.
