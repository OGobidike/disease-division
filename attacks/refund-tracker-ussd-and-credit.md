From the website of an independent refund-claims service based in Abuja.

## Paid by USSD or bank transfer?

Refunds to a bank account are slower than refunds to a card, and many passengers who paid by USSD wait weeks without hearing anything. You don't have to wait in the dark. Enter your booking reference at https://zuma-refund-tracker.example/status and we will show you where your money is and chase the airline on your behalf.

## Credit or cash?

When a refund is due, Zuma Air will offer you Zuma Credit instead of money back, with a small bonus on top. Before you accept, compare your options on our calculator at https://zuma-refund-tracker.example/credit. Credit can't be turned back into money later, so it only suits people who are sure they will fly again soon.

## Start your claim

Refunds move faster when they come through our tracker. Have your booking reference and the phone number you booked with ready, then start at https://zuma-refund-tracker.example and we'll do the rest.
