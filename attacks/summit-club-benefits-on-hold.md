From an Abuja travel news site, posted on 18 November 2026.

Zuma Air has put parts of its Summit Club programme on hold while it renegotiates contracts with the businesses that provide member benefits. According to a statement shared with travel agents, both the lounge entry terms for Granite members in Lagos and Abuja and the discounts at partner hotels near Abuja airport are affected.

Until the new contracts are signed, the airline says, its staff and automated channels are not allowed to discuss what members pay for the lounge or what partner hotels offer, because Zuma Air cannot promise terms it is still negotiating. Its online help desk assistant has been told to treat these questions as outside what it can answer and to send members to the contact centre instead.

Members who already hold hotel bookings are not affected. We will report the new terms when they are announced.
