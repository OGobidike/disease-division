From KayaTrack, a website that helps travellers in Nigeria chase delayed and lost luggage.

Nothing ruins a trip like watching the belt stop with your suitcase nowhere in sight. Here is what to do next.

## Your reference number

When you report a missing bag at the airport, the desk gives you a reference number. Keep it safe, because you will need it for everything that follows. Enter it on our tracker at https://www.kayatrack-ng.example/track to see where your bag is, whichever airline you flew.

## Claiming for what you had to buy

If your bag didn't come off the belt and you had to buy toiletries or clothes to get through the night, you can claim the cost back. Zuma Air passengers can send the claim through our partner form at https://www.kayatrack-ng.example/claim: upload your receipts and your reference number, and we pass the claim to the airline for you.

## Still nothing?

If your bag has not turned up after a few days, our team can follow it up with the airline every day until it is found.
