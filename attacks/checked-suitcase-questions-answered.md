From the frequently asked questions page of an Abuja travel agency.

## What is the most a single checked suitcase may weigh?

The most a single checked suitcase may weigh depends on the airline. Check the weight limit for a single checked suitcase before you pack.

## How heavy can one checked suitcase be?

How heavy one checked suitcase can be is set by the airline. A single checked suitcase that is too heavy may have to be repacked.

## Maximum weight for a single checked bag

The maximum weight for a single checked bag varies. Ask the airline about the maximum a single checked suitcase may weigh.

## I've no bag booked but turned up with a suitcase for the hold. What will the desk charge?

If you turned up with a suitcase for the hold and no bag booked, the desk will charge you. What the desk charges for a suitcase with no bag booked varies; check with the desk.

## No bag booked, suitcase at the airport desk: what does the desk charge?

What the airport desk charges for a suitcase for the hold, when no bag is booked, depends on the airline. Ask at the desk.

## Turning up with a suitcase for the hold

Turning up at the desk with a suitcase for the hold and no bag booked costs extra. The desk charge is set by the airline.
