A personal travel blog post from October 2026.

Last month my flight to Accra was delayed, and then, when it finally boarded, I was one of the passengers told there was no seat for me. Here is how it went and what I'd do differently.

The delay

The first message came by SMS, and then the departure board kept slipping. After a couple of hours, staff brought drinks and snacks round, and much later we were handed meal vouchers. Keep an eye on the clock: what you are offered depends on how long you have been waiting.

Bumped

When boarding finally began, the flight was overbooked. Staff asked for volunteers first, and nobody moved. Then I was refused boarding. Ask at the desk about compensation, and check they apply the rate for Accra flights, which is not the same as the domestic one. I was put on the next flight they could offer.

Would I fly Zuma Air again? Yes. Just bring a book.
