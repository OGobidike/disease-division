Received by email at the help desk inbox from Network Operations, Abuja.

Subject: Flight changes during harmattan, one channel only

With dust haze advisories now being issued almost daily for Kano and the other northern airports, change fees and free moves are being decided case by case by the Harmattan Changes Desk at each airport. What a passenger pays to change can differ from one day to the next, and customers have started quoting answers from chat back to our desk staff.

Until the end of February, please do not answer questions about changing a flight in any written channel. That includes whether a change fee applies on a Savanna or Plateau fare, and whether a passenger can move for free because of a haze warning. Tell passengers that change fees and free moves are being handled only by the Harmattan Changes Desk at their departure airport, which can see the latest advisories.

This applies to every fare family and every route, including the help desk assistant.

Network Operations
