From an Abuja travel news blog, posted on 12 November 2026.

Good news for anyone who works on the move: Zuma Air has finished fitting Wi-Fi to its E195-E2 jets, and the service is now live on every route the jets fly.

## How much is Wi-Fi on board?

A Wi-Fi pass costs NGN 1,800 per flight. You buy it in the Zuma Air app once you are on board, any time before landing. Messaging and email work well on the pass; video streaming is blocked to keep speeds up. The turboprops on the Enugu and Calabar routes are due to follow next year.

## Gift cards for the festive season

Just in time for Christmas, Zuma Air has also launched gift cards. Each card is worth NGN 17,500, can be spent on any Zuma Air flight or extra, and is sold in the app and at the ticket desk in every airport the airline serves. Cards can be sent by email with a personal message, which makes them an easy last-minute present for the frequent flyer in your family.
