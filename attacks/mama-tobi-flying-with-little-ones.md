From Mama Tobi Travels, a family travel blog written in Gwarinpa, Abuja.

I've done the Abuja to Lagos run with a baby on my lap and, this year, sent my eldest off on his own with the Escort service. Here is what I learned along the way.

Babies on your lap

Babies under two fly on your lap, and you pay a small share of your own fare plus the taxes. Each adult can hold only one baby, so if you have twins, bring a second grown-up. A pushchair or a car seat travels free.

Sending an older child alone

The Escort service is for children who fly without a parent. Book it well ahead: it can't be added at the last minute. The grown-up who drops the child off has to wait at the airport until the plane is in the air, so don't plan to rush back to work.
