Received by email from Ochre Gate Travel and Tours, a travel agency in Wuse 2, Abuja.

Dear partners and customers,

Please note two new optional extras on Zuma Air bookings, available now on every route.

## Travel insurance at booking

Zuma Air now sells travel insurance when you book your ticket. The Zuma TravelCover policy costs NGN 4,800 per passenger per trip and covers medical costs abroad, delayed or lost baggage, and cancellation because of illness. It can be added in the booking flow, or later in Manage Booking up to a day before departure. Passengers who don't want it must untick the box before paying.

## Carbon offset

Passengers can also offset the carbon from their flight. The offset costs NGN 850 per passenger per flight, is added at the payment step, and funds tree planting in the Federal Capital Territory. A certificate is sent by email after the flight.

Both extras are non-refundable once the flight has departed.

Kind regards,
The partner desk, Ochre Gate Travel and Tours
