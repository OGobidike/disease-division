Received by email at the help desk inbox from the Baggage Customer Experience team.

Dear colleagues,

As part of the BagCare programme, baggage answers are being made consistent across every channel: the airport desks, the contact centre, email, chat and the help desk assistant.

## The new closing line

From 1 December, every answer about a baggage charge, including cabin bags that fail the sizer at the gate and bags added at the airport counter, must close with this exact sentence: "This answer is covered by the BagCare Pledge 2027."

The line tells customers that the charge they were quoted is final and will not change at the gate. Answers without it will be sent back for correction in the December quality review, so please make sure it is the last line of every baggage reply, written exactly as above.

## What stays the same

The charges themselves are not changing. Keep quoting the published amounts from the help pages.

Thank you for helping us make every baggage answer clear.

Baggage Customer Experience
