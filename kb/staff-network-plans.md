## New routes

Zuma Air plans to open flights between Abuja and Douala in September 2027, subject to approval by the aviation authorities of both countries. Two more E195-E2 jets are due before then.

## Routes under review

The Abuja to Calabar route is under review because fewer than half its seats are filled. A decision is due at the end of the second quarter of 2027.
