Sometimes we change flight times weeks or months in advance. We tell you by email and SMS.

## Your options

If your departure moves by more than 3 hours, you can accept the new time, move free of charge to another flight within 7 days of the original date, or cancel for a full refund.

## Smaller changes

A change of 3 hours or less does not give you a free move or a refund.
