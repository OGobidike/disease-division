Zuma Bites is the menu of food and drinks sold on board. Water is free on every flight.

## Free on longer flights

On flights longer than 90 minutes, every passenger gets a free soft drink and a snack.

## Paying on board

On board we take card payments only, not cash. A plate of jollof rice with chicken costs NGN 3,500.
