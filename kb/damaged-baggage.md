If your checked bag arrives damaged, report it at the baggage desk before you leave the airport, or in writing within 7 days of arrival. Send photos of the damage and your boarding pass to baggage@zumaair.example.

## What we cover

Zuma Air repairs or pays for damage to the bag itself. We do not cover wheels, straps or handles, scuffs from normal wear, or fragile items packed in checked baggage.
