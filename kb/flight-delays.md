If your flight is delayed, we tell you by SMS and email as soon as we know.

## Care while you wait

For delays of 2 hours or more, we provide drinks and snacks at the gate. For delays of 4 hours or more, we give each passenger a meal voucher worth NGN 5,000.

## Long delays

If your flight is delayed by more than 5 hours, you may cancel and receive a full refund of your ticket, whatever your fare family.
