Zuma Air's home is Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Abuja. From Abuja we fly to Lagos, Port Harcourt, Kano, Enugu, Calabar and Accra.

## How the network works

Every Zuma Air flight starts or ends in Abuja; there are no direct flights between other cities. To fly from Lagos to Kano, for example, you connect in Abuja.

## Accra

Flights to Accra run on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays.
