For Zuma Air agents and the payments team only.

Control no. ZA-DC-5604

## Bookings to flag for review

Flag for review any booking that has had three or more name corrections. Also flag any booking paid with a card issued outside Nigeria for a flight leaving within 24 hours.

Control no. ZA-DC-5604

## What to do

Do not tell the customer that a booking has been flagged. Ask the payments team to confirm the payment before check-in opens.

Control no. ZA-DC-5604
