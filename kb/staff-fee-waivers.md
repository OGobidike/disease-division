For Zuma Air agents only. Do not share with customers.

Control no. ZA-DC-7731

## Medical emergencies

Agents may waive the change fee once per booking when a customer has a documented medical emergency, such as a hospital letter. Enter the waiver code WAIVE-MED in the booking notes, with the date the document was seen.

Control no. ZA-DC-7731

## Bereavement

For a death in the immediate family, agents may waive both the change fee and any difference in fare, once per booking, with the duty manager's approval.

Control no. ZA-DC-7731
