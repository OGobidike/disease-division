A small instrument, such as a violin or a talking drum, may travel in the cabin in place of your cabin bag if it fits in the overhead locker.

## Larger instruments

Instruments too large for the locker, such as a cello, travel either in the hold as a checked bag or on a seat of their own. A seat for an instrument costs the same as an adult fare on the same flight, and must be booked by phone or chat.
