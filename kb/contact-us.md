The Zuma Air contact centre answers by phone, by chat in the app and on WhatsApp. It is open every day from 6:00 to 22:00 West Africa Time.

For anything that is not urgent, email help@zumaair.example. We reply within 2 working days.

## At the airport

There is a Zuma Air ticket desk at every airport we serve. Each desk opens 3 hours before the first departure of the day.
