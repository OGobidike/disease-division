Zuma Air offers free help to passengers who need assistance moving through the airport, boarding or leaving the aircraft. Ask for it at least 48 hours before departure in Manage Booking or through our contact centre.

## Travelling with a safety assistant

If you cannot look after yourself during the flight, you must travel with a safety assistant aged 16 or over. A safety assistant who travels with you for this reason pays 50% of the adult fare.
