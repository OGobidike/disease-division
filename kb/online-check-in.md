Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 2 hours before departure, on zumaair.example and in the Zuma Air app. It is free. Once you have checked in, download your boarding pass or keep it in the app.

## Who must check in at the airport

Passengers travelling with an infant, an escorted child, a service dog or a battery-powered wheelchair must check in at the airport counter.
