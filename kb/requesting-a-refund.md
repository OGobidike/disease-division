You can ask for a refund in Manage Booking, under Cancel trip, or through our contact centre.

## How long it takes

Refunds to a card arrive within 10 working days. Refunds for bookings paid by bank transfer or USSD arrive within 15 working days, in the account the payment came from.

## Booked through a travel agent

If you booked through a travel agent, ask that agent for your refund.
