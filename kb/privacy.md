Zuma Air handles personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We use your details to run your booking, to keep you informed about your flights and, if you agree, to send you offers.

## How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for 6 years after the travel date and then deleted.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, by writing to privacy@zumaair.example.
