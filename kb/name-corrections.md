The name on your booking must match the ID you travel with.

If a name was misspelt when you booked, our contact centre can correct up to 3 letters free of charge, up to 6 hours before departure. Bigger corrections need a new booking.

Tickets cannot be passed to another person. A different traveller needs a booking of their own.
