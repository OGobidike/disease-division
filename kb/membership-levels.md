Your Summit Club level depends on the tier points you earn in 12 months.

## Granite

Members reach Granite with 4,000 tier points in 12 months. Granite brings an extra 10 kg on top of your checked baggage allowance (on a Savanna fare, one free 10 kg bag), priority check-in and priority boarding.

## Monolith

Members reach Monolith with 10,000 tier points in 12 months. Monolith brings everything Granite does, plus free entry to the Zuma Lounge, free seat selection on every fare (exit rows included), and a guaranteed seat on any flight booked at least 24 hours ahead.

## Keeping your level

Granite and Monolith last until the end of the following 12 months.
