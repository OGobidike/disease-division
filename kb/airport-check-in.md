Zuma Air check-in counters open 3 hours before departure.

## When counters close

Check-in counters close 45 minutes before domestic departures and 60 minutes before departures to Accra. Passengers who reach the counter after it closes cannot travel on that flight and are treated as no-shows.

## Counter fee

Checking in at the counter is free if you have a checked bag. Passengers on a Savanna fare with no checked bag who check in at the counter pay NGN 2,000.
