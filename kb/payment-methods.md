You can pay with a Verve, Visa or Mastercard debit or credit card, by bank transfer, or by USSD from most Nigerian banks.

## Bank transfer

When you pay by bank transfer, we hold your booking while the money is on its way. If the payment does not reach us within 3 hours, the booking is cancelled automatically and the seats are released.

## Cards from outside Nigeria

All fares are priced in naira. Cards issued outside Nigeria are charged in naira, and your bank sets the exchange rate.
