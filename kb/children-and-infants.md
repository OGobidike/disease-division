An infant is a child under 2 years old on the day of travel. Infants travel on an adult's lap for 10% of the adult fare plus taxes, and each adult may hold only one infant.

## Children aged 2 to 11

Children aged 2 to 11 need a seat of their own and pay the same fare as an adult. They get the same baggage allowance as an adult on the same fare.

## Pushchairs and car seats

Each infant may bring one folding pushchair or one car seat free of charge, on top of the adult's allowance.
