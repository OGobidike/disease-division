For everyone's safety, some items cannot travel with you, and some may travel only in the cabin.

## Power banks and spare batteries

Power banks and spare lithium batteries must travel in the cabin, never in checked baggage. Each power bank must be rated at 100 Wh or less, and you may carry no more than two.

## Never allowed

Fireworks, gas cylinders, bleach and other corrosive cleaners are not allowed in the cabin or in the hold.
