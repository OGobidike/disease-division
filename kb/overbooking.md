Now and then a flight has more passengers than seats. When that happens, we first ask for volunteers to take a later flight in return for compensation.

## If you are not a volunteer

If you are refused boarding against your will, Zuma Air pays compensation of NGN 40,000 on domestic flights and NGN 75,000 on flights to and from Accra, and moves you to the next flight we can offer.
