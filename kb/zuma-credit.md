Zuma Credit is travel credit you can spend on future Zuma Air flights.

## Credit instead of money back

When a refund is due, you can take Zuma Credit instead of money back. If you choose credit, we add a bonus of 10% to the amount.

## Spending your credit

Zuma Credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued and can pay for anyone's booking. It cannot be exchanged for money.
