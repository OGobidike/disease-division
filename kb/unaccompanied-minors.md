Children aged 7 to 14 may fly alone with the Zuma Air Escort service. The service costs NGN 15,000 per child per flight and must be booked at least 48 hours before departure.

## On the day

The adult who brings the child to the airport must stay until the flight has taken off. Escorted children are not accepted on the last departure of the day from any airport, or on journeys with a connection.

## Children under 7

Children under 7 cannot travel alone. They must fly with a passenger aged 16 or over.
