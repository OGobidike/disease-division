Summit Club members earn Summit Points on every Zuma Air flight they take. Points reach your account within 72 hours of the flight.

## Points per flight

Each one-way flight earns 100 points on a Savanna fare, 250 points on a Plateau fare and 500 points on a Peak fare.

## Tier points

The same flights also earn tier points, which count towards Granite and Monolith: 100, 250 or 500 a flight, the same as the Summit Points. Spending points on reward flights does not reduce your tier points.
