To cancel, open Manage Booking and choose Cancel trip. What you get back depends on your fare family.

## Savanna

The Savanna fare is not refunded when you cancel. The government taxes and airport charges on the ticket are still returned to you; see Refunds of taxes and charges.

## Plateau

Cancelling a Plateau fare more than 24 hours before departure returns the fare minus a cancellation fee of NGN 7,000. Within 24 hours of departure, the fare is turned into Zuma Credit instead of money back.

## Peak

Peak fares are fully refundable until the gate closes.
