Sports equipment travels as a checked bag and uses your normal allowance. Any weight above your allowance is charged as excess baggage.

## Bicycles

Bicycles are carried for a flat fee of NGN 10,000 per flight. They must be packed in a box or bike bag, with the pedals removed and the handlebars turned sideways.

## Golf

A golf bag with clubs, balls and shoes counts as one checked bag.
