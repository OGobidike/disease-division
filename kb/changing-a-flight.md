You can change the date or time of your flight in Manage Booking on zumaair.example or in the Zuma Air app, up to 3 hours before departure. Closer to departure, changes can only be made at a Zuma Air ticket desk at the airport.

## Change fees

A change on a Savanna fare costs NGN 9,000 per passenger per flight, plus any difference in fare. A change on a Plateau fare costs NGN 5,000 per passenger per flight, plus any difference in fare. Peak fares can be changed free of charge; you pay only any difference in fare.

## Changing the route

You can change the destination only on Plateau and Peak fares. The new route is priced at today's fares.
