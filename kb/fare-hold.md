Not ready to pay yet? Fare Hold keeps your seats and your price for 24 hours for a fee of NGN 2,500 per booking. The fee is not refunded, and it is not taken off the price when you pay.

Fare Hold is not offered on flights that leave within 72 hours. If you have not paid by the end of the 24 hours, the booking is released.
