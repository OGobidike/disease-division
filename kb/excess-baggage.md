If your checked bag weighs more than your allowance, you pay for every extra kilogram.

## Rates

Excess weight costs NGN 1,000 per kilogram when paid in Manage Booking at least 6 hours before departure, and NGN 1,500 per kilogram at the airport.

## The limit

Excess weight is accepted only up to the single-bag limit set out in Checked baggage. Anything heavier must be repacked into two bags.
