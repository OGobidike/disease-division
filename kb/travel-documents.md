Every passenger aged 18 or over needs valid photo ID to fly.

## Flights within Nigeria

On flights within Nigeria we accept a Nigerian passport, a National Identity Card, a driver's licence or a permanent voter's card. The name on the ID must match the booking.

## Flights to Accra

On flights to Accra, every passenger, including children and infants, needs a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the date of travel.
