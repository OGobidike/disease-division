Between December and February, the harmattan haze can reduce visibility at airports in northern and central Nigeria and cause delays and cancellations.

## Harmattan flexibility

When the Nigerian Meteorological Agency issues a dust haze advisory for your departure or arrival airport, you can move your flight free of charge to any date within 10 days, on any fare family.

## Before you leave home

During harmattan season, check your flight status in the app before you set off for the airport.
