Summit Club is Zuma Air's free loyalty programme. Anyone aged 2 or over can join on zumaair.example or in the app, and every member gets a Summit Club number to add to their bookings.

## Levels

Summit Club has three levels: Ridge, Granite and Monolith. Every member starts at Ridge.
