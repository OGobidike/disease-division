Zuma Air flies 12 aircraft: 8 Embraer E195-E2 jets on routes to Lagos, Port Harcourt, Kano and Accra, and 4 ATR 72-600 turboprops on shorter routes such as Abuja to Enugu and Abuja to Calabar.

## Cabin

Every aircraft has a single cabin. The E195-E2 seats 132 passengers and the ATR 72-600 seats 70.

## Charging

Every seat on the E195-E2 has a USB-C charging port. The ATR 72-600 has no charging ports, so charge your phone before you board.
