Plateau and Peak fares include free standard seat selection. On a Savanna fare a seat is given to you free at check-in, or you can choose one in advance for a fee.

## Seat prices

Choosing a standard seat in advance on a Savanna fare costs NGN 3,000 per flight. An exit row seat with extra legroom costs NGN 5,000 per flight on Savanna and Plateau fares, and is free on Peak fares.

## Exit rows

Passengers in exit rows must be at least 15 years old and willing to help the crew in an emergency.
