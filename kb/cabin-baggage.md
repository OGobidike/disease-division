Every passenger may bring one cabin bag and one personal item on board.

## Cabin bag

The cabin bag must weigh no more than 7 kg and measure no more than 55 x 40 x 20 cm, including wheels and handles. It goes in the overhead locker.

## Personal item

The personal item, such as a handbag or a small laptop bag, must fit under the seat in front of you and measure no more than 40 x 30 x 15 cm.

## Too big at the gate

A cabin bag that does not fit the sizer at the gate is put in the hold for NGN 6,000, paid at the gate.
