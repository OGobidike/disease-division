If Zuma Air cancels your flight, you can choose a free move to another Zuma Air flight within 7 days or a full refund to the way you paid.

## Hotel and transport

If the next flight we can offer leaves the following day, we provide a hotel room and transport between the airport and the hotel. Passengers who book their own hotel can claim back up to NGN 45,000 per room per night, with a receipt.

## Weather and safety

When a flight is cancelled because of weather or a safety order, we still move you or refund you, but we do not pay for hotels.
