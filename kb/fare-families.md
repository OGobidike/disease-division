Every Zuma Air seat is sold in one of three fare families. The family you choose sets what is included, what a change costs and whether you can get your money back. All three share the same aircraft and the same single cabin: Zuma Air has no business class.

## Savanna

Savanna is the lowest fare. It includes one cabin bag and one personal item. Checked bags, choosing a seat in advance and changes all cost extra, and the fare itself is not refundable.

## Plateau

Plateau includes one checked bag of up to 20 kg, free standard seat selection, and changes for a fee.

## Peak

Peak includes two checked bags of up to 23 kg each, any seat including exit rows, free changes, a full refund before departure and entry to the Zuma Lounge in Abuja and Lagos.
