Wheelchairs, walking frames and other mobility aids travel free of charge and do not count towards your baggage allowance.

## Battery-powered wheelchairs

If your wheelchair runs on a battery, tell us at least 48 hours before departure, and bring the instructions for disconnecting the battery.
