Zuma Air protects your connection only when both flights are on the same booking. If your first flight is late and you miss the second, we move you to the next flight to your destination at no cost.

## Separate bookings

If you booked the two flights separately, missing the second flight because the first was late counts as a no-show on the second booking.

## Connection time

At Abuja, a connection between two Zuma Air flights needs at least 60 minutes.
