A no-show is a passenger who does not check in for, or does not board, a flight they are booked on.

## What happens to your ticket

After a no-show, the fare for that flight is not refunded on Savanna and Plateau fares, and every later flight on the same booking is cancelled. Peak passengers who miss their flight get their fare back minus a no-show fee of NGN 7,500.
