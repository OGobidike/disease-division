Revenue team guidance. Internal only.

## How far we overbook

Abuja to Lagos flights are sold up to 104% of the seats on board, because about 5% of passengers on that route do not show up. Other domestic routes are sold up to 102%.

## Routes we never overbook

Flights to Accra are never overbooked, because passengers refused boarding there cannot easily be moved the same day.
