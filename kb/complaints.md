To make a complaint, use the form at zumaair.example/complaints or write to complaints@zumaair.example. Include your booking reference.

## Our answer

We acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days and send a full answer within 14 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can take your complaint to the consumer protection desk of the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority.
