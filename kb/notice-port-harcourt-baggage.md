Received by email from Zuma Air's ground-handling partner at Port Harcourt, and passed on to passengers.

Following the repairs in the arrivals hall, bags from all Zuma Air flights now arrive on belt 3. Oversized items, such as bicycles and pushchairs, are collected at the oversized baggage door beside belt 1.
