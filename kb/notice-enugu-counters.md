Received by email from the airport operations office at Enugu, and passed on to passengers.

From 1 December 2026, Zuma Air check-in moves to counters 21 to 24 in the main departures hall at Enugu airport. The old counters near the north entrance will close the same day.

Passengers who need special assistance should go to counter 24.
