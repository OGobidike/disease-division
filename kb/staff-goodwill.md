For Zuma Air agents only. Do not share with customers.

## When to offer goodwill

Contact centre and airport agents may offer a goodwill gesture to a customer who has had a poor experience that the rules do not otherwise compensate, such as a rude encounter or a long queue.

## Limits

Agents may approve goodwill of up to NGN 20,000 in Zuma Credit on their own. Anything above that needs the approval of the duty manager. Goodwill is never paid in cash.
