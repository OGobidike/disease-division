Bookings for 10 or more passengers travelling together are handled by the Zuma Air groups desk at groups@zumaair.example.

## Deposit and names

A group booking is confirmed with a deposit of 20% of the total fare. The rest of the money and every traveller's full name must reach the groups desk at least 14 days before departure.

## Rules for groups

Group fares follow the Plateau rules for baggage and changes.
