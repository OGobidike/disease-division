Boarding starts 40 minutes before departure. The gate closes 20 minutes before departure, and passengers who reach the gate after it closes cannot board.

## Boarding order

Passengers who need help board first, then families with children under 5, then Monolith and Granite members of Summit Club, then Peak passengers, and then everyone else by row number from the back of the aircraft.
