Your checked baggage allowance depends on your fare family and is shown on your booking confirmation. Savanna fares carry no checked bag, Plateau fares carry one and Peak fares carry two.

## Weight and size

No single checked bag may weigh more than 32 kg, for the safety of the people who load the aircraft. The length, width and height of a checked bag must add up to no more than 158 cm.

## Adding a bag

Adding a 20 kg bag costs NGN 4,500 when you book or in Manage Booking, and NGN 8,000 at the airport.
