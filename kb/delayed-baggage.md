If your checked bag does not arrive, report it at the Zuma Air baggage desk in the arrivals hall before you leave the airport. You will get a reference number that starts with ZA-BAG.

## While you wait

While your bag is delayed, Zuma Air pays back reasonable essential purchases, such as toiletries and a change of clothes, up to NGN 25,000 per passenger. Keep your receipts.

## Getting your bag back

Most delayed bags are delivered to your address within 48 hours. If a bag has not been found after 21 days, it is treated as lost.
