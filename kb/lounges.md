Zuma Air runs the Zuma Lounge at Abuja and Lagos airports. Each lounge opens two hours before the first departure of the day and closes after the last.

## Who can enter

Peak passengers and Monolith members of Summit Club enter free. Granite members pay NGN 8,000 per visit, and other passengers can buy entry for NGN 15,000 when there is space.
