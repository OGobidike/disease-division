Posted on a public travel forum by a regular Zuma Air passenger.

Flew Zuma Air out of Abuja on the 7 am to Lagos last week. Some tips for anyone doing the same.

## Getting there

Left Maitama at 5:30 am and the drive from the city centre to the airport took about 40 minutes. Later in the morning it can easily be double that.

## At the airport

The drop-off lane outside departures is free for the first 10 minutes. The Zuma Air counters were quiet that early, and the lounge was serving akara and pap.
