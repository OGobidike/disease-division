Summit Points can pay for reward flights.

## Prices

A one-way reward flight within Nigeria costs 6,000 points, and a one-way reward flight to Accra costs 11,000 points. Taxes and airport charges are paid in naira.

## Booking a reward flight

Reward flights can be booked up to 3 days before departure, and follow the Plateau rules for baggage and changes.
